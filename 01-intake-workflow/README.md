# 01 · Intake Workflow

## Automated Client Intake Workflow

From initial inquiry to organized records and a timely acknowledgment.

![Automated Client Intake Workflow](Bevora_Intake_Workflow.png)

### How it works

1. **Access the intake form:** The client visits the website or opens a direct intake link. Both routes lead to one online form.
2. **Submit information:** The client completes and submits the form.
3. **Record intake information:** Submitted details are captured in a centralized intake register.
4. **Transfer information:** An automated integration sends the details to the contact management system.
5. **Create a contact record:** A contact profile is created using the submitted information.
6. **Send an acknowledgment:** The client automatically receives confirmation of receipt.

### Example acknowledgment

> Thank you for submitting your intake information. Our team has received it and will contact you about the next steps.

### Value

Connects intake submissions, organizes contact information, and automatically acknowledges each submission.
